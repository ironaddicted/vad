Before you begin

Your bathroom remodeling questions.

Have a specific question about your home? Tell us what you are planning.

What affects the cost of a bathroom remodel?

Room size, demolition, tile coverage, waterproofing, fixtures, glass, and layout changes affect cost. Existing moisture damage and needed repairs can also change the scope. We identify confirmed work and unresolved conditions in the estimate.

Can you replace a bathtub with a shower?

We can discuss a tub-to-shower conversion and assess the available space, drainage, plumbing, waterproofing, and glass requirements. Final scope depends on the existing bathroom and your selections.

How long will my bathroom be unavailable?

Bathroom downtime depends on the work, inspections where required, curing times, deliveries, and glass installation. We discuss access to another bathroom and confirm the proposed sequence before work starts.

Can you help with a bathroom that has leaks or damaged tile?

Tell us about visible leaks, staining, loose tile, or soft flooring. Photos help start the discussion, but concealed conditions may need an on-site assessment or investigation. New tile alone does not resolve an underlying waterproofing problem.
