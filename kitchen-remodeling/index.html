Before you begin

Your kitchen remodeling questions.

Have a specific question about your home? Tell us what you are planning.

What affects the cost of a kitchen remodel?

The size of the kitchen, cabinet scope, countertop and tile selections, existing conditions, and changes to plumbing, electrical, or layout all affect cost. We prepare a project-specific estimate rather than promise a one-size-fits-all price.

Can I update the kitchen without replacing everything?

Yes. Depending on the condition of the room, a focused cabinet, backsplash, flooring, or finish update may suit your goals. Tell us what you want to keep so we can discuss practical options.

How long will my kitchen renovation take?

Timing depends on scope, selections, material availability, and any required trade work or approvals. We discuss access and kitchen downtime and confirm scheduling when the work and materials are defined.

Should I buy cabinets or appliances before contacting you?

You can share products you are considering, but confirm dimensions, compatibility, supply responsibilities, and lead times before ordering. Photos of your kitchen are enough to begin the conversation.
