Website privacy notice

Updated October 29, 2026.

Estimate inquiries

When you request an estimate, we use the name, phone number, email address, and project details you provide to respond and discuss your project. Our inquiry service processes the submission. The inquiry may include the service page and campaign reference that brought you to the site.

Website measurement

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads tags to measure website activity, advertising performance, and successful estimate requests. These services may use cookies and similar identifiers. We measure events such as page engagement, estimate-form interactions, successful inquiries, and clicks on our phone and text links. A click on a phone link is not confirmation that a call took place.

Our custom Analytics event parameters do not include your form's name, email, phone number, or project message. Google services process device, browser, and advertising identifiers under their own policies. See how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google's advertising controls.

Project photos and chat

Photos you send to discuss a project are used for that discussion. Do not include information you do not want us to receive. The optional project chat on other pages has a separate privacy notice.

Contact

For privacy questions or to ask about information you submitted, call or text (704) 750-9110. Avoid sending sensitive information through project forms.
